Yas Free Box - Jira Tasks (5-Day Sprint)
========================================
Project: MysteryBox
Date: 2026-08-10

Display rules:
- First Mixx visit: 6 boxes (1 free Yas + 5 pay)
- Later visits: 5 pay boxes only

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPIC: Yas Free Box Design & Data - Day 1
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MB-1 | Confirm Yas free-box rules
One free open per subscriber via APP when source=mixx_app|yas_app; separate prizes from normal boxes.

MB-2 | Create Mixx prizes table
Store Yas-managed prize amounts, probabilities, and enable flags.

MB-3 | Create Mixx eligibility / usage table
Track per-MSISDN free-box eligibility and first-open used status.

MB-4 | Add Yas free box type in config
Register Yas Free Box separately from Bronze/Silver and other pay boxes.

MB-5 | Agree APP source=mixx_app|yas_app handoff
Confirm how Mixx App passes source so portal/API can detect first vs later visit.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPIC: Mixx Award, Open & Payout - Day 2
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MB-6 | Award free Yas free box on first Mixx visit
If source=mixx_app|yas_app and unused, create/award free Yas free box alongside 5 pay boxes.

MB-7 | Implement Mixx-specific open flow
Open free Yas free box without payment using separate prize engine.

MB-8 | Select Mixx prize by probability
Pick prize from Mixx prize config according to configured probabilities.

MB-9 | Mark free Yas free box as used after open
Update eligibility so free box is not offered again.

MB-10 | Wire Mixx prize disbursement
Send airtime/wallet payout for Yas free-box wins via existing disbursement path.

MB-11 | Keep 5 pay boxes available always
Ensure pay boxes remain listed on first and later Mixx visits.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPIC: Yas Admin Prize Control - Day 3
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MB-12 | Add Yas staff permission for Mixx prizes
Restrict Mixx prize management to authorized Yas/admin staff.

MB-13 | Implement Mixx prize list API
Return configured Mixx prizes with amounts, probabilities, and status.

MB-14 | Implement Mixx prize create/update API
Allow Yas to add/edit prize amount, probability, and enable/disable.

MB-15 | Add audit log for Mixx prize changes
Record who changed which Mixx prize config and when.

MB-16 | Provide admin UI or API usage docs
Simple admin screens or Postman-ready docs for Yas team.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPIC: Portal UX (6 then 5) - Day 4
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MB-17 | Detect source=mixx_app|yas_app on portal load
Identify Mixx App visits and drive box listing from eligibility state.

MB-18 | First Mixx visit shows 6 boxes
Display 1 free Yas free box + 5 pay boxes for eligible first visit.

MB-19 | Later Mixx visits show 5 pay boxes
Hide free Yas free box after it has been used; show pay boxes only.

MB-20 | Handle free-box used / ineligible states
Return clear status when free Yas free box is already used or not available.

MB-21 | Align portal box APIs for Mixx listing
Ensure box-list endpoint returns 6 or 5 boxes based on Mixx eligibility.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
EPIC: Test, UAT & Go-Live - Day 5
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

MB-22 | E2E first Mixx visit flow
Verify first visit shows 6 boxes, free open works, prize is awarded.

MB-23 | E2E later Mixx visit flow
Verify later visit shows only 5 pay boxes and free box is not shown.

MB-24 | Regression non-Mixx path
Confirm SMS/APP non-Yas free box flows remain unchanged.

MB-25 | UAT with Yas and APP teams
Walk through first/later visit, prize admin, and payout with stakeholders.

MB-26 | Deploy and smoke Mixx payouts
Ship to production and smoke-check award, open, and disbursement.

MB-27 | Monitor Yas free-box logs
Watch eligibility, open, prize selection, and payout errors post go-live.
