MysteryBox - Box Prices & Prizes (Simple Overview)
==================================================
Date: 2026-09-16
Currency: Tanzanian Shillings (TSh)

These are the prices customers pay, and the prizes they can get from each box.


--------------------------------------------------
1. SHABA (Bronze)
--------------------------------------------------
Buy price: TSh 300
Or open free with: 100 points
Chance of extra prize: 2%

Always get:
- Loyalty points: 5 to 20

May also win (if lucky):
- Cash: TSh 500 to 1,000
- Airtime (salio): TSh 500 to 1,000


--------------------------------------------------
2. FEDHA (Silver)
--------------------------------------------------
Buy price: TSh 500
Or open free with: 200 points
Chance of extra prize: 2%

Always get:
- Loyalty points: 10 to 30

May also win (if lucky):
- Cash: TSh 1,000 to 3,000
- (Phone / 5G router prizes are currently off)


--------------------------------------------------
3. DHAHABU (Gold)
--------------------------------------------------
Buy price: TSh 1,000
Or open free with: 300 points
Chance of extra prize: 2%

Always get:
- Loyalty points: 20 to 50
- Entry into the daily draw (DROO ya siku) worth TSh 100,000

May also win (if lucky):
- Cash: TSh 1,000 to 5,000
- (Phone / gadget / 5G router prizes are currently off)


--------------------------------------------------
4. PLATINUM
--------------------------------------------------
Buy price: TSh 5,000
Or open free with: 400 points
Chance of extra prize: 1%

Always get:
- Airtime (salio): TSh 1,000 to 5,000
- Loyalty points: 30 to 70
- Entry into daily draw (TSh 100,000)
- Entry into weekly draw (TSh 500,000)
- Entry into monthly draw (TSh 1,000,000)

May also win (if lucky):
- Cash: TSh 5,000 to 8,000
- (Phone / gadget / 5G router prizes are currently off)


--------------------------------------------------
5. ALMASI (Diamond)
--------------------------------------------------
Buy price: TSh 10,000
Or open free with: 500 points
Chance of extra prize: 1%

Always get:
- Airtime (salio): TSh 1,000 to 5,000
- Cash: TSh 1,000 to 20,000
- Loyalty points: 50 to 100
- 5 entries into daily draw (TSh 100,000 each)
- Entry into weekly draw (TSh 500,000)
- Entry into monthly draw (TSh 1,000,000)

May also win (if lucky):
- Feature phone (enabled)
- (Smartphone / tablet / smartwatch / 5G router prizes are currently off)

NOTES
--------------------------------------------------
- Higher box price = bigger prizes.
- "Always get" means the customer receives these every time they open that box.
- "May also win" means there is a small chance of an extra prize.
- BillPay / Malipo box is free (TSh 0) and is not sold in the normal customer list.
- Renewal currently awards a Shaba (Bronze) box opportunity.
- Draw entries put the customer into daily, weekly, or monthly prize draws.
